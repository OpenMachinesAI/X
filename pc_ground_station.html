Tuning Notes

No yaw vanes are used. Pitch/roll stabilization is by the four 40 mm EDFs only. Yaw drift over 50 degrees triggers automatic abort and motor shutdown on the Uno, with the abort code sent back here through the phone.

Sluggish: raise Rate P, then Angle P. Overshoot/rocking: lower Angle P or add a little Rate D. Fast buzz: lower Rate P/D. Persistent lean: add tiny Rate I.
