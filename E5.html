01

RSP recovery system

Rocket Servo Parachute (RSP) is E5’s servo-operated parachute recovery system. Designed to release the parachute after ascent, RSP provides servo-operated recovery for the return to the ground.

02

Nosecone parachute door

A dedicated door is integrated into the grey nosecone/fairing. It provides the opening for parachute deployment through the side of the upper body.

03

Otter flight computer

Otter is E5’s onboard flight computer, with a 12-sensor suite for motion, pressure, temperature and heading measurements. Its sensors support observation of the rocket’s movement and surrounding conditions during flight.

Acceleration measurements describe changes in motion; the gyroscope measures rotation. Barometric pressure can support altitude estimation, while the temperature sensor and compass provide temperature and magnetic-heading measurements.

05

Airframe & fins

The airframe, nosecone and all four fins are made from PLA (polylactic acid). A cylindrical white body tube joins the grey pointed fairing. Four black fins are arranged around the lower body to provide passive aerodynamic stabilization. A black aft band completes the lower body.
