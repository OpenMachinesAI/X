What is more accurate in this version?

Rapier rigid bodyPosition, velocity, rotation, inertia and ground collision are integrated by Rapier.
Real force locationsMotor thrust is applied at each selected engine mount, so off-axis forces create torque.
Changing mass & inertiaPropellant depletion updates the rigid body's mass properties through the burn.
3D aerodynamicsDrag acts opposite air-relative velocity, including horizontal wind.
Attitude-aware predictionThe simulated landing predictor reduces usable vertical thrust when the vehicle is tilted.
Fixed timestepThe physics world advances in small fixed increments instead of depending on display frame rate.
